Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple Intermediaries Reduce Farmers' Earnings and Increase Consumer Prices.

Problem Creator's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution
Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

Proposed Solution
Our proposed solution is a digital marketplace called FarmConnect.
- It directly connects farmers with consumers and bulk buyers.
- Farmers can sell their products without depending on middlemen.
- Consumers can buy fresh products at affordable prices.
- The platform provides delivery and logistics support.
- AI helps predict product demand and suggest better delivery routes.
  
Innovation and Uniqueness:
FarmConnect reduces middlemen and makes agricultural trading easier, faster and more transparent.
Technical Approach

Technologies Used:
- Frontend: HTML, CSS, JavaScript
- Backend: Python, Django
- Database: MySQL
- AI: Machine Learning
- Maps: Google Maps API
  
Working Process:
1. Farmers register on the platform.
2. Farmers upload their product details and prices.
3. Consumers browse and order products.
4. AI predicts demand and helps manage stock.
5. Logistics partners deliver products.
6. Farmers receive better earnings.
   
Feasibility and Viability
- The platform can be developed using existing technologies.
- Farmers can easily register and upload products.
- Internet access is required to use the platform.
- Delivery and transportation may be challenging.
- We can overcome this by partnering with local delivery services.
- The platform can be expanded to different villages and cities.
  
Impact and Benefits
For Farmers:
- Better income.
- Direct access to customers.
- No need for multiple middlemen.
  
For Consumers:
- Fresh products.
- Affordable prices.
- Easy online purchasing.
  
For Society:
- Reduces food wastage.
- Improves agricultural development.
- Creates employment opportunities.
- Makes the supply chain more efficient.
